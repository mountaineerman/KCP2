Lessons learned
-LED brightness is important (some are too bright, some are too dim).

HARDWARE
Fixed/mitigated:
-USB cable still provides power to the Arduino Mega even when the 12V power switch is turned off.

Potential Fixes:
-Mach Stepper Motor range slightly less than gauge
-Heading Gauge Stepper Motor idle high-pitch tone
-Paint touchups
-Bluetooth keyboard sometimes disconnects from computer, possibly because of interference from metal casing. Add a cable port and inner-panel extension cable. Consider a 'permanent' keyboard.

"Won't Fix" Design Improvements:
-Module connector locations are a bit awkward. Shift positions, lengthen cables to make it easier to connect/disconnect.
-#0-80 screws which attach the x27 stepper motors to their adapters are too long. Better to screw into a nut than to cut a thread into plastic.
-Remove two middle holes on the Panel Mounting Plate (behind keyboard) for aesthetics.
-Move the 2 holes in the Back Plate above the Dodge Plate up (machining error or CAD model error).
-#4-40 screws are too big for the stepper driver board holes and LED driver board holes.
-Arduino Mega USB cable and power cable conflict with the LED Driver board position.
-LED holder holes are slightly too big (LEDs fall out of holders).
-NEMA17 Stepper Motor: 1mm closer mounting holes along length.
-Add Fuse Holder to CAD model.
- Tolerances for some of the module plates (Module E, F, etc.).
